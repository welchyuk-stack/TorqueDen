## Reel — Hiding nav bar demo (Stories + Reels)

- 0–2s: Feed open, nav bar visible. Text overlay: "Your feed."
- 2–7s: Slow scroll down — nav bar slides away, photos go full screen. Overlay: "Full screen."
- 7–10s: Scroll up, nav returns. Overlay: "Only when you need it."
- End card: "TorqueDen v1.2 — free on the App Store. No account needed to browse."

