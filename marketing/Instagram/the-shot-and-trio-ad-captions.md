## Notes for boosting as an ad

- Both captions lead with a hook line in the first sentence — Meta truncates longer captions in the ad unit, so the strongest line needs to land before the cut.
- CTA is explicit ("Download," "Free on the App Store") since paid reach won't have the same follow-through as organic.
- Keep hashtags in the first comment, not the caption, for the ad — cleaner primary text, and Meta's ad review sometimes flags hashtag-heavy primary text.
- If Ads Manager asks for separate "Primary text" vs "Headline" fields: use the first line as headline (e.g. "Think you've got The Shot?" / "Discover · Build · Belong"), and the rest as primary text.
