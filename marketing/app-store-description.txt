TorqueDen is the digital garage-clubhouse for car people — the place to show your build, log every mod, find meets near you, and talk builds with people who actually get it.

YOUR GARAGE, YOUR WAY
Every car you own gets its own page: a full spec sheet, a build log organised by real categories, and a story that stays attached to the car — not scattered across old posts. Track the daily, the project that's been sat on jack stands for months, and everything in between.

REACT LIKE A CAR PERSON, NOT A STRANGER
Skip the generic like button. Fire, Clean, Sick, Insane — react with whichever one actually fits what you're looking at.

DISCOVER BUILDS LIKE YOURS
Search by make, model, or exact chassis code — "GC8," "E46," "AE86" — to find the builds that actually match what you drive. Flip on "Near me" and the same search narrows to your area.

FIND THE MEET. OR START ONE.
Browse what's on near you week by week, drop a pin on the live map, and see who's going before you commit. RSVP "going" or "maybe" and it's saved to My Meets. Starting your own takes a minute — a title, a date, and an address is all it takes.

FORUMS FOR ANYTHING — NOT JUST A MAKE
Marque forums like Subaru, Honda, BMW, Volkswagen, and Audi come built in, but anyone can create a forum of their own, on any topic. Make it public or private, and moderate it yourself.

NO RANK, NO GATEKEEPING
Builds aren't ranked by spend or mod count. A stock daily and a full restomod both belong here equally.

Free to join. Built by car people, for car people.
