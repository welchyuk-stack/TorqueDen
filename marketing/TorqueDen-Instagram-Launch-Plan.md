
  Link-in-bio → `torquedenapp.github.io` (the landing page) with a mailto/waitlist CTA.

- **Highlights (create as content lands):** Feed · Discover · Build Log · Clubs · FAQ

## 2. Voice on Instagram

Same voice as the app — plain-spoken, enthusiast-to-enthusiast, never corporate or hype-y. Captions talk like someone in the garage, not a marketing team.

- Do: "Nice work — what's next on it?" energy.
- Don't: "Leverage our platform," rank builds by spend, or overpromise.
- Every post earns its place with a real build, a real spec, or a real answer — no filler.

## 3. Content pillars

| Pillar | What it is | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **Build spotlight** | A single car/build, real spec sheet or mod log | 2x/week |
| **App preview** | Screens of the actual app (Discover, Build Log, Clubs) framed as "what it looks like inside" | 1x/week |
| **Community question** | Ask-the-crew prompts ("Stage 2 and climbing — what's next?") to drive comments | 1x/week |
| **Build-in-public** | Behind the scenes of making TorqueDen — decisions, features shipping, why photos-only at launch | 1x/week |
| **Meme/culture** | Light car-culture humor, in-voice, no stock imagery | as-needed |

## 4. Posting cadence (first 4 weeks)

Aim for 3–4 feed posts/week + daily Stories once the account is live. Reels used for build reveals and quick app walkthroughs (15–30s, captions on-screen, no music licensing risk — use trending audio only after checking usage rights).

## 5. Hashtag sets

Rotate 3 sets so the account doesn't look automated. Mix broad + niche, 15–20 tags per post, first comment not caption.

**Set A (broad car culture):**
`#carsofinstagram #jdm #stancenation #cargram #carbuild #modifiedcars #garagelife #cartok #enthusiast #carporn #cargarage #dailydriven #carmeet #boosted #turbo`

`#buildlog #carspecs #garagebuild #wrenchtime #stage2 #tuned #ownersclub #carcommunity #showyourbuild #modlife #carfam #trackday #liveryidea #carphotography`
