# TorqueDen — Reel Script v2
**Vertical 1080x1920, ~20s, organic or ad variant**

---

## Scene 1 — Hook (3s)
**Visual:** Someone scrolling feed, stops on a build post, taps Fire reaction

**On-screen text:** "No 'like' button."

**Voiceover/Caption:** React the way you actually talk about cars.

---

## Scene 2 — Problem (4s)
**Visual:** Quick cuts of phone screens showing generic social apps (Instagram, TikTok, Reddit)

**On-screen text:** "Instagram won't let you find your exact car."

**Voiceover/Caption:** Search M2, G87, E46 — find builds that match YOUR car.

---

## Scene 3 — Meet Up (4s)
**Visual:** Meets list → map view → someone RSVPs going

**On-screen text:** "Stop scrolling group chats."

**Voiceover/Caption:** Find meets near you. See who's going before you commit.

---

## Scene 4 — Build Log (3s)
**Visual:** Garage tab → build log → categories (engine, suspension, wheels)

**On-screen text:** "Every mod. Logged."

**Voiceover/Caption:** Your build's whole story in one place.

---

## Scene 5 — Community (3s)
**Visual:** Forums tab → Subaru/Honda/BMW forums → thread with discussion

**On-screen text:** "Built by car people."

**Voiceover/Caption:** Talk builds with people who actually get it.

---

## Scene 6 — Outro (2–3s)
**Visual:** TorqueDen logo + app icon

**On-screen text:** "TorqueDen" / "Show it. Track it. Meet up." / "Free on the App Store"

**Voiceover/Caption:** Download now.

---

**Total: ~19s**

**Notes:**
- No Ken Burns zoom — quick cuts, snappy pacing
- Each scene = one feature benefit (not "here's the feature," but "here's why you need it")
- Hook immediately with the reactions system (it's the most unexpected/memorable thing)
- CTA is soft ("Download now") — can hardcode "Link in bio" if running as ad
- Works for organic posts AND paid ads
