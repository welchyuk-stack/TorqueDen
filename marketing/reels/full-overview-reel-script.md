# TorqueDen — Full app overview Reel / Meta ad script

Vertical 1080x1920, ~20s, mixes your real screen recording (Feed) with the rest
of the app on remaining scenes. Silent — add a trending sound in Reels, or a
licensed track in Ads Manager.

| # | Length | Visual | On-screen text | Source |
|---|--------|--------|-----------------|--------|
| 1 | 13s | Feed — full scroll into opening the reaction picker and tapping Fire | **"Show your appreciation"** | Your real screen recording (trimmed to the scroll + first react) |
| 2 | 3s | Discover — search + "Near me" | **"Find builds near you"** / Search by make, model, or chassis code | Simulator screenshot |
| 3 | 3s | Build tab — mod categories, "Add update" | **"Log Every Mod"** / Track your build from stock to show car | Existing captioned asset |
| 4 | 3.5s | Meets — live map + weekly list | **"Find what's on. Meet the community."** / Browse near you, week by week | Simulator screenshot |
| 5 | 3s | Forums — Subaru/Honda/BMW etc. | **"Share the knowledge"** / Post, help out, talk builds | Simulator screenshot |
| 6 | 3.5s | Outro card — logo, headline, QR | **"TorqueDen"** / Show it. Track it. Meet up. / "Search TorqueDen on the App Store" | Generated card |

**Total: ~27s**

Notes:
- Scene 1 is your actual footage — the full scroll through to the reaction picker and the tap on Fire, kept as one continuous take. No synthetic zoom; just cropped to fit the vertical frame.
- Scenes 2–5 get a slow Ken Burns zoom since they're static screenshots. Captions are composited as a fixed overlay *after* the zoom, so the text itself never pans or scales with the background — it stays put and readable the whole time.
- Scene 3 is built from the existing build-log asset with its old caption cropped off, then given the same fixed caption treatment as everything else, for consistency.
- Outro card is fully static (no zoom at all) so the CTA and QR are never in motion.
- Crossfades between every scene, no hard cuts.
- Outro card now points at the live App Store listing (apps.apple.com/us/app/torqueden-car-builds-mods/id6788811379) — the beta/TestFlight QR has been retired now that the app is approved and public.

If you record real footage for Discover, the build update, Meets, or Forums later, send it over and I'll swap those scenes in for the synthetic ones.
