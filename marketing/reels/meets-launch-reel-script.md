# TorqueDen — "Meets" launch Reel / Meta ad script

Vertical 1080x1920, ~19s, silent (add a trending sound in Reels, or a licensed
track in Ads Manager). Same cut works as an Instagram Reel and as a Meta/Facebook
ad — no dates or platform-specific UI baked in, just the product.

| # | Length | Visual | On-screen text |
|---|--------|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | 2.5s | Home feed screen | **"Still finding meets in group chats?"** |
| 2 | 3.5s | Meets screen — live map + weekly list | **"Find what's on near you"** / See what's happening, week by week |
| 3 | 3s | Meet detail screen — RSVPs, organiser | **"See who's going"** / RSVP before you commit |
| 4 | 3s | Meet detail screen — address + date | **"Or start your own"** / Title, date, address — done in a minute |
| 5 | 3s | Forums screen | **"Plus forums for anything"** / Not just a make |
| 6 | 3.5s | Outro card — logo, headline, QR | **"TorqueDen"** / Meets. Forums. Your garage. / "Join the beta — scan to start" |

**Total: ~18.5s**

Notes:
- Ken Burns zoom on every scene (slow zoom-in on 1–3, zoom-out on 4–5) keeps it feeling alive since there's no motion in the source screenshots.
- Crossfade between scenes, no hard cuts — matches the calmer, "premium" feel of the rest of the brand rather than punchy jump-cuts.
- No voiceover; text carries the message so it works muted (most feed/ad views are sound-off).
- Outro card reuses the same TestFlight QR from the printed cards, so all channels point to one link.
