# TorqueDen — Fast & Furious Parody Reel Script
**Vertical 1080x1920, ~25s, organic content**

---

## Scene 1 — Opening (4s)
**Visual:** Someone looking at their phone, slightly confused expression

**Voiceover (Dom-style):** "You know what the most important thing is? It's not the car. It's not the wins."

**Text on screen:** "It's the build log."

---

## Scene 2 — The Reveal (3s)
**Visual:** Cut to phone screen — TorqueDen app opens, goes straight to a car profile

**Voiceover:** "Hector's running Spoon engines..."

**On-screen text:** "Hector's Car" (or mock name)

---

## Scene 3 — Build Log (5s)
**Visual:** Scroll through build log categories:
- Engine (Spoon K-series highlighted)
- Turbo/Supercharger section
- Suspension
- Wheels
- Intake/Exhaust

**Voiceover:** "...nitrous system..."

**UI shows:** Mod progression, dates, comments from other users reacting

---

## Scene 4 — Comments/Reactions (4s)
**Visual:** Comments section fills with reactions (Fire, Clean, Sick, Insane)

**Voiceover:** "...everything about this car is custom."

**On-screen text:** "Fire 🔥" reactions populate

---

## Scene 5 — The Flex (5s)
**Visual:** Gallery of the actual car photos in the build (car meet photo, dyno, stance shot)

**Voiceover:** "This is not just a car. It's a build."

**On-screen text:** "Every mod. Logged."

---

## Scene 6 — Outro (3s)
**Visual:** TorqueDen logo + app icon

**On-screen text:** "TorqueDen" / "Show your build. Log every mod. Meet the community." / "Download free"

**Voiceover:** "That's what TorqueDen is for."

---

**Total: ~24s**

---

## Production Notes

**Legal/Platform:**
- This is a parody/homage, so it lives in a gray zone with copyright. Safe for organic (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) — less safe for paid ads (Meta/Google might flag it)
- Voiceover should sound *inspired by* the original, not a direct recording/quote — paraphrasing is safer
- The humor lands with car people who know the scene

**Creative Tips:**
- Use a real build as "Hector's car" (could be one of your early users' builds, with permission)
- The build log should show actual progression (not just mods, but dates/photos showing the journey)
- Comments section filling with Fire reactions should feel organic/natural
- The vibe should be: "Dom's talking about a car, but instead of the movie, he's showing you TorqueDen"

**Best Use:**
- Organic TikTok/Instagram post (not ads)
- Tag car/tuning communities
- Will resonate with JDM/street racing culture audiences
