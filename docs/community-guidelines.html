Community Guidelines

Effective 19 July 2026

TorqueDen is a community for car enthusiasts to share builds, mods, and a passion for motorsport. To keep it a place worth being, everyone who uses TorqueDen — posting, commenting, messaging, or creating clubs — agrees to these rules.

What TorqueDen is for

  • Sharing your build journey — mods, progress, setups, and track days
  • Connecting with other enthusiasts who share your passion
  • Discovering cars and builds you wouldn't otherwise find
  • Respectful discussion about anything automotive

The rules

1. Be respectful
Disagree with someone's build choices all you like — but attack the idea, not the person. Harassment, personal insults, and targeted abuse are not tolerated, including in direct messages.

2. Keep it automotive
Posts and clubs should relate to cars, bikes, motorsport, or related lifestyle. Off-topic spam will be removed.

3. No hate speech
Content that demeans people based on race, gender, religion, nationality, sexual orientation, or disability has no place here, ever.

4. No explicit content
This is a car community. Keep photos and language work-safe. Graphic violence and sexual content will be removed immediately.

5. No spam or unsolicited promotion
Don't flood the feed with repetitive posts, unsolicited adverts, or referral links. Clubs exist for communities, not mass marketing campaigns.

6. No dangerous or illegal content
Don't post content that encourages illegal street racing, dangerous driving, or anything that puts other people at risk. Documenting track days and legal spirited driving is absolutely fine.

7. Be honest
Don't impersonate other users or misrepresent your build. Fake specs, stolen photos, and fake accounts will be permanently removed.

8. Respect privacy
Don't post personal information about other people without their consent — addresses, phone numbers, or anything they haven't chosen to share publicly.

Enforcement — two-strike system

We operate a two-strike policy. Moderators assess each report individually; the outcome depends on severity.

Strike What happens
1st The offending content is removed and you receive a warning notification. Your account is flagged.
2nd Your account is permanently banned and all content is removed. This decision is final.

Immediate ban: Certain violations — hate speech, sexual content involving minors, doxxing, or coordinated harassment — result in a permanent ban without a prior warning.

We aim to review all reports within 48 hours.

How to report

Tap the ··· menu on any post, comment, club, or profile and choose Report. In direct messages, use the ··· button in the top-right of the conversation. We review every report.

Clubs

Club owners and moderators are responsible for keeping their clubs within these guidelines. Clubs that consistently host rule-breaking content may be archived or removed.

Contact

Questions about a moderation decision, or something that needs urgent attention? Email us at torquedenapp@gmail.com. We'll get back to you as soon as we can.
